About SetupDrop

Random accessories don't build an identity.

SetupDrop is a Pakistan-based desk setup lifestyle concept built around one simple idea: accessories should be designed together. Instead of selling unrelated desk products, SetupDrop releases complete themed Drops where desk mats, keycaps, cables and accent pieces share one visual language.
